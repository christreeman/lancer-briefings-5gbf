# Mission // #101
## Make Land at Pallas

You members are freshly graduated lancers stationed on the **U.N. The Bleak December**. Your orders are to stay on standby while the ship completes its journey to **Pallas**. Stay alert, as this is the edge of the colonized universe. The navy, these colonists, and you are all entering the unknown. You carry these hopes and dreams on your back.

# Goals
- Exit Near-Light Speed Travel at the Rao Co Blinkgate
- Blink Space Travel to **The Long Rim**
- Begin Near-Light Speed Travel to the destination planet of **Pallas**

# Stakes
- The lives of the entire colony are in your hands. Should the ship's defenses fail, **you** are the last resort.

# Reserves
- None at this time.
